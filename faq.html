🙋 Frequently Asked Questions

Do I or my staff need to answer calls to manage this calendar?
No. The infrastructure operates 100% via automated self-service. When a property owner needs to book an appointment or wipe a date slot, they process it completely on the page. The software updates your grid directly.
How does this data connect to my existing field crew?
During onboarding, you securely link your corporate email calendar profile (Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar). Our deployment matrix hooks your software asset node straight to your devices so alert items sync on autopilot.
What is your cancellation framework?
We run a fixed, transparent utility tier. There are no fast-talking sales agents or phone customer service queues. If you wish to suspend operations, email a simple notice parameter to info@vortexsonicmetrics.com and your subscription database file closes instantly.
